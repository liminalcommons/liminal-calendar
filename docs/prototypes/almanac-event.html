The lamplit hours

0006121824 UTC

The lamp is lit while Lisbon, São Paulo, New York and San Francisco are all awake. If you set a gathering outside it, you're writing in the dark: that's allowed, it's just quieter.

Once a month, when the moon is full, someone lights the small fire at the Hearth and we sit around it for two hours. Bring something to burn: a note, a worry, a draft of something you've been meaning to finish. Nothing is recorded. The only rule is that you stay until the fire goes down.

Latecomers are welcome, and so is leaving quietly. If you have wood, bring wood. If you have a song, it will be asked for.

A ritual is an event that keeps its place on the calendar because people keep coming, not because someone schedules it.
