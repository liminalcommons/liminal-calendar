Show & Tell — The Process

The hour is the anchor. Everything else is plumbing — built so the practice can run without a dedicated organizer, on a 14-day cycle that loops back into itself.

Principles

  • Self-organizing. Slots are claimed, not assigned. No host curation.
  • Consent by default off. Nothing is published unless presenters explicitly opt in after the session.
  • The system does the organizing; humans do the facilitating. If no human shows up to facilitate, the structure carries the room.
  • Residue feeds the next cycle. Each session's archive becomes recruitment for the one after.

The 14-day cycle

T −14d
System

Open the next session

Date is auto-scheduled (biweekly Thursday 7pm). 4 slots open. An optional theme is seeded by the LLM from the residue of the previous session.

T −13d
System · Newsletter

Recap + invitation

LLM-generated newsletter goes to subscribers: highlights from last session (consented posts/videos only), next session's theme, "4 slots open" with a one-click claim link.

T −14d → T −3d
Presenter

Claim a slot

Anyone can claim one of the 4 slots. AI-assisted submission helps sharpen what the 10 minutes is really about. Slot state: open → claimed → confirmed.

T −3d
System · Reminder

Lineup announcement

Newsletter goes out with the 4 confirmed Topics + presenters. Subscribers get a calendar invite. Empty slots stay open until T-1d.

T −1d
System · Reminder

Presenter check-in & RSVP nudge

Presenters get a personal reminder with their sharpened Topic summary. RSVP'd attendees get tomorrow's link.

T · 0:00
System · Room

The hour opens itself

Room link goes live. Built-in timer runs the structure: 15 min warm-up (LLM-seeded prompt) → 4 × 10 min Topics → 5 min breakout. Recording starts automatically. No facilitator strictly required.

T +1d
Presenter

Consent prompt

Each presenter receives a private link with: their recording, an AI-drafted post from the transcript. They choose, per artifact: share video / share post / share my name. Default for all: not shared.

T +2d
System · Archive

Publish consented residue

Whatever was consented to becomes a post in the public archive. Anonymous if name not shared. The post is the seed of the next newsletter.

T +14d
System

Loop

The cycle restarts. The previous session's archive is the recruitment material for the next one.

↻ The practice runs itself; humans show up if they want to.

Who does what

The system does

  • Schedule sessions (biweekly, automatic)
  • Open and close slots
  • Send newsletters & reminders
  • Sharpen submitted Topics (LLM)
  • Open the room, run the timer, record
  • Draft post from transcript
  • Collect consent & publish archive

Humans do

  • Claim a slot when something itches
  • Show up (or don't)
  • Talk for 10 minutes
  • Decide what gets shared
  • Optionally facilitate the hour (welcomed, not required)
