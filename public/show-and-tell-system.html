Liminal Calendar · A Practice

Show & Tell — the system that carries it —

A biweekly 60-minute community meeting where submitted ideas get presented. Submission stays open between sessions — sign in to submit, edit later, and keep track of what you brought. The meeting is recorded; clips and posts are made by hand and distributed to the channels we already use.

What the app does

Submission

A form on /show-and-tell/submit — sign in to submit so you can revisit and edit your Topic before the session. The form captures a title, description, optional banner, and which channels you're OK with the recording going to.

The pool

/show-and-tell shows the next meeting time and the running list of submitted ideas. Anyone can see what's been brought in.

The meeting itself

A biweekly recurring event in the calendar (60 min, Wed early evening), rendered on the Week and Month views like any other event. Held in Castalia.

What stays manual

Editing the recording

After each meeting, the host edits the recording into clips, descriptions, and short posts. Done in whatever editing tool the host already uses. The app doesn't touch this.

Cross-posting

The host posts to YouTube, the Facebook Metamodern group, related Telegram groups, Hylo Limicon groups, and the newsletter — by hand, on each platform. No app automation.

Why manual on purpose

Building cross-posting automation is weeks of work for marginal time savings. Run the practice 3–5 cycles first. If a specific friction becomes intolerable (e.g. "I keep forgetting which channels each idea went to"), build only that.

The practice keeps itself; subscribers show up if they choose to.
